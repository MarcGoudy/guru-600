# Prophet-600 v2022 Patch Collection 1 (sounds 101 to 200 of the Guru-600)

**Source.** `P600_v2022_Patch_Collection_1.syx` and `.pdf` come unchanged from `P600_v2022_Patch_Collection_1.zip`, published by the
GliGli Prophet-600 firmware project with its v2022 release: https://github.com/image-et-son/p600fw/releases/tag/v2022

**Authors.** The patch authors named in the collection's PDF: Andy, el-folie, imogen, Jpro600k, Matrix12x (and the original
factory-based "Z80" patches it lists).

**Licence.** The p600fw project states: "Everything is under GPL v3 license, except files that have their own license in the
header" (its README). The collection carries no licence of its own, so it is redistributed here under that project's GPL v3,
like the rest of the Guru-600.

**Use in the Guru-600.** `p600/make_bank.py` converts these 100 programs into the firmware's places 101 to 200
(`firmware/src/p600_bank.h`); four names cut to 11 characters were shortened so they stay distinct (Orch. 1 to 3, Sp.Oboe Ch.).
